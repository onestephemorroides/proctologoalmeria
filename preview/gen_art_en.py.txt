c = re.sub(r'<meta name="description" content="[^"]*"',

           '"image": "https://www.proctologoalmeria.com/img-condilomas-anales-hero.png"',

c = re.sub(r'"datePublished":\s*"[^"]*"', '"datePublished": "2026-07-27"', c, count=1)
c = re.sub(r'"dateModified":\s*"[^"]*"', '"dateModified": "2026-07-27"', c, count=1)

c = re.sub(r"<h1>.*?</h1>", f"<h1>{TITULO}</h1>", c, count=1, flags=re.S)

<p>When someone comes in with anal warts, they have usually noticed one or more lesions on the skin around the anus. <p>This is why an apparently successful treatment can be followed by a recurrence within weeks. It is not that the treatment failed: lesions were left untreated where nobody looked.</p>

<p>Anoscopy is a simple examination that allows the inside of the anal canal to be seen. <p>Warts are not stable lesions. They grow and multiply over time, and that is where the real difference lies between coming in early or late.</p>

<h3>Few, small lesions</h3>
<p>These are resolved by electrofulguration <strong>in the clinic itself, under local anaesthesia</strong>, in a single session. You walk home the same day and return to your usual activity immediately.</p>

<h3>Extensive or confluent lesions</h3>
<p>When the affected area is large, the outpatient approach is no longer viable. It then requires <strong>an operating theatre, general or spinal anaesthesia</strong> and, frequently, <strong>several sessions</strong> spread over time so as not to compromise healing in such a delicate area. The postoperative period is considerably more uncomfortable and recovery takes longer.</p>

<p>Put another way: what today is a twenty-minute procedure in clinic can, within a few months, become a surgical operation requiring admission. <strong>The difference is not determined by the severity of the disease, but by how much time is allowed to pass.</strong></p>

  <div class="article-cta-box">

    <a href="anal-warts.html" class="related-card">
